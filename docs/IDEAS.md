# Ideas & Backlog

Not in scope until promoted into `PLAN.md`. Add freely; note the "why".

## Personalisation (the reason this exists)
- **About-me + per-relationship context** — who I am, my role, what I care about; per-person notes
  ("Sam prefers async, owns billing") fed into enhancement and chat.
- **Auto-picked templates** — choose the template from calendar title/attendees/history.
- **Voice of the notes** — style settings (terse bullets vs narrative), learned from my edits to past
  enhancements.
- **People pages** — auto-built from meetings: last talked, open threads, commitments both ways.
- **Topic threads** — follow a topic ("pricing", "hiring") across meetings; timeline view.

## Assistants & agents
- **Custom assistants** — named agents with their own instructions + tools (e.g. "Sales coach",
  "PM brief writer"), shareable within a workspace.
- **Live in-meeting help** — suggested questions, "you haven't asked about budget", definitions of jargon.
- **Post-meeting autopilot** — draft follow-up email, file action items in Linear, post summary to Slack.
- **Weekly digest** — scheduled agent: decisions made, open commitments, meetings to prep for.
- **Pre-meeting brief** — 10 min before a call: who's attending, what we last discussed, open items.
- **Oat as an MCP server** — expose my meetings/notes to Claude and other agents.
- **Decision log** — extract decisions across meetings into a living page.

## Capture
- Local-only transcription (Whisper / Parakeet on Apple Silicon) for private meetings.
- Speaker diarization within the "them" channel (multiple remote speakers).
- In-person mode (mic only, diarized).
- Dictation / voice memos into any page.
- Opt-in encrypted cloud backup of the local audio archive (and cross-device playback).
- Re-transcribe old meetings from the archive with a better/local model.
- Audio clips: share a 30-second snippet around a transcript segment.
- Consent helper: auto-message in chat "I'm taking AI notes" per platform.
